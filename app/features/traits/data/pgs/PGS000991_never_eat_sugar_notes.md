# Never eat sugar tendency

Consumer-facing nutrition trait based on PGS000991.

Read this as a cautious panel-relative signal related to sugar-avoidance tendency, not as a direct measurement of current diet.

Important limits:

- No imputation is used.
- No ancestry-adjusted normalization is used.
- Percentiles are relative to the local reference panel.
- Real-world sugar intake depends on culture, routine, food access, habits, health goals, and personal choice.
- This is not dietary or medical advice.
