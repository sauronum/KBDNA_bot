Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing physical trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited body-fat-percentage tendency, not as a direct estimate of current body composition.
- Sex, age, hormones, nutrition, training, illness, medication use, and hydration can all move real-world body fat percentage away from the polygenic signal.
- Consumer copy should keep this as a cautious relative body-composition signal rather than turning it into a stronger claim about current appearance, fitness, or metabolic health.
