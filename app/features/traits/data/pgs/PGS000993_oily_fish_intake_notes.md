# Oily fish intake tendency

Consumer-facing nutrition trait based on PGS000993.

Read this as a cautious panel-relative signal related to oily-fish intake tendency, not as a direct measurement of current diet or omega-3 status.

Important limits:

- No imputation is used.
- No ancestry-adjusted normalization is used.
- Percentiles are relative to the local reference panel.
- Real-world fish intake depends on culture, region, food access, price, religion, allergies, taste, and personal habit.
- This is not dietary or medical advice.
