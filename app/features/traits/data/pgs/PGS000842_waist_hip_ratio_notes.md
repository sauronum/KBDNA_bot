Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing physical trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited body-shape tendency, not as a direct statement about current waist size, hip size, or visible silhouette.
- Sex, age, hormones, training, nutrition, and current body composition can all move real-world waist-to-hip ratio away from the polygenic signal.
- The result should stay in the lane of cautious relative interpretation, not medical advice about abdominal fat or cardiometabolic risk.
