Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing physical trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited tendency toward relatively higher or lower appendicular lean mass within the current panel, not as a direct estimate of visible arm or leg muscle size.
- Training status, nutrition, age, sex hormones, illness, medication use, and hydration can all move real-world lean mass away from the polygenic signal.
- The source phenotype is narrower than whole-body lean mass, so consumer copy should stay specific and avoid turning it into a broad promise about body shape or athletic ability.
