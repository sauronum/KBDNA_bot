Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing lifestyle trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited tendency toward earlier or later daily timing preference, not as a direct statement about current bedtime or wake time.
- Work schedule, school schedule, caregiving, artificial light exposure, age, social routine, and accumulated sleep debt can all move real-world behavior away from the polygenic signal.
