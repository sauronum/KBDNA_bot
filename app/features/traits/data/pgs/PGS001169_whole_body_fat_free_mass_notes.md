Practical note for this project:

- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited tendency toward relatively higher or lower lean mass within the current panel, not as a direct estimate of current muscle size, athletic ability, or gym response.
