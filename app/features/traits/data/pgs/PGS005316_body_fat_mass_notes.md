Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing physical trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited tendency toward relatively higher or lower body fat mass within the current panel, not as a direct estimate of current body fat percentage.
- Diet, physical activity, age, sex, hormones, medication use, illness, and hydration can all move real-world body composition away from the polygenic signal.
- Consumer copy should keep this as a cautious body-composition tendency rather than turning it into a promise about current appearance, fitness, or metabolic health.
