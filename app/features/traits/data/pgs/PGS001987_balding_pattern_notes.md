Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing appearance trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited tendency toward patterned scalp-hair loss, not as a direct statement about current visible baldness.
- The source phenotype is male-pattern balding, so age, sex, hormones, treatment history, and grooming choices all matter a lot for real-world appearance.
- This should stay a cautious appearance trait, not a guarantee about whether someone will or will not lose hair.
