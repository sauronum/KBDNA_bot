Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing appearance trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as a cautious inherited tendency toward younger-looking facial ageing, not as a direct estimate of current visible age.
- Real-world facial ageing is strongly influenced by sun exposure, smoking, sleep, stress, skincare, weight change, health, and image conditions.
- This trait needs especially careful wording so it stays a relative appearance signal rather than sounding like a guaranteed anti-ageing outcome.
