Practical note for this project:

- This is the strongest consumer-facing lifestyle trait currently bundled for the no-imputation raw-array workflow.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `38` samples out of `39` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Raw-array overlap on individual files can still be only moderate, so `confidence` may remain `low`.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited tendency toward more or less coffee consumption, not as a direct statement about current cups per day.
- Culture, habit, work schedule, sleep, stimulant preferences, and health-driven behavior can all shift real-world intake away from the polygenic signal.
