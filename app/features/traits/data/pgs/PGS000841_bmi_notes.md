Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing physical trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `34` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation is strongest when framed as an inherited BMI-related tendency, not as a direct statement about current weight, current body composition, or metabolic diagnosis.
- Diet, activity, age, illness, medication use, socioeconomic context, and many other non-genetic factors can move real-world BMI away from the polygenic signal.
- BMI is itself a blunt body-size measure and should not be confused with a direct estimate of body fat, muscle mass, or health quality.
