Practical note for this project:

- This is a workable consumer-facing social trait for the current strict raw-array workflow without imputation, but the interpretation must stay cautious and non-clinical.
- The local empirical reference artifact currently validates successfully and includes `33` samples out of `34` total raw files screened for the panel.
- Product interpretation should frame this as an inherited tendency related to self-reported loneliness or subjective social disconnection, not as a diagnosis and not as proof of current social isolation.
- Real-world loneliness is shaped strongly by relationships, culture, health, recent events, and life stage, so this trait should stay clearly separated from direct statements about current well-being.
