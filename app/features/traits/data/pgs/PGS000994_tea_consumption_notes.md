# Tea intake tendency

Consumer-facing nutrition trait based on PGS000994.

Read this as a cautious panel-relative signal related to tea-intake tendency, not as a direct measurement of current tea, caffeine, or hydration habits.

Important limits:

- No imputation is used.
- No ancestry-adjusted normalization is used.
- Percentiles are relative to the local reference panel.
- Real-world tea intake depends on culture, region, household habits, caffeine tolerance, work routine, and personal choice.
- This is not dietary or medical advice.
